# ViralVibe YouTube Shorts operating plan

- Channel: https://www.youtube.com/@ViralVibe-1m-g1y
- Content direction: mixed viral entertainment
- Format: YouTube Shorts only
- Publishing policy: prepare and queue content automatically, but publish/schedule only after the channel owner approves the specific video and its metadata.
- Initial planning cadence: 2 Shorts per day as a test target; review after 14 days using YouTube Analytics before increasing or reducing volume.
- Default creative format: vertical 9:16, original edit, strong first-second hook, clear story/payoff, readable captions, sound design, and a concise end beat.
- Content pillars:
  1. Funny and surprising moments recreated with original narration/editing.
  2. Heartwarming and feel-good moments.
  3. Interesting facts, mini-stories, and unexpected reveals.
  4. Light challenges, rankings, and relatable entertainment.
- Rights and safety: use original footage, properly licensed stock, or footage with documented permission. Do not re-upload other creators' clips just because they are trending. Add meaningful original commentary/editing; avoid misleading claims, dangerous challenges, and synthetic impersonation.
- Production pipeline: topic research -> originality/rights check -> script -> voice -> licensed stock footage -> edit/effects -> captions -> title/description/tags -> preview -> owner approval -> scheduled upload -> analytics review.
- Upload defaults: private until an approved future publish time is set. Scheduled uploads must use a future ISO-8601 publish time and the YouTube API's private scheduling behavior.
- Required operational gates: channel OAuth must be verified against the expected channel ID; each asset must be stored in the trusted media store; every publish job must carry explicit owner approval.
- Current state: this file records the operating plan only. It does not mean Google OAuth, worker deployment, media generation, or a live publishing schedule has been configured.

## First 14-day experiment

- Publish up to 2 approved Shorts daily.
- Rotate across the four content pillars rather than posting unrelated clips without a consistent format.
- Track views, viewed-vs-swiped-away, average view duration, retention, likes, comments, shares, and subscribers gained per Short.
- Keep the strongest hooks and story structures; retire formats that repeatedly underperform.
