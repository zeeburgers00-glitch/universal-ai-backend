- Spec samples are concepts, not published work; label them "spec concept" and do not use logos or copyrighted media without permission.
- Spend $0 until approved. Campaign spend ceiling: USD 100/month; provider billing limits must also be configured separately.

## Five personalized spec concepts (not yet produced or sent)

### 1. TAB x TAB — brunch and creator collaboration
**Hook:** "Your Westbourne Grove weekend starts here."  
**15–20 sec storyboard:** exterior/location cue → close-up of brunch plate → tea/coffee pour → baked-goods detail → warm table/ambience → end card: "Plan your next brunch".  
**Caption draft:** "A little pause in Westbourne Grove: brunch favourites, specialty tea and coffee, and something freshly baked. Save this for your next London catch-up. [Confirm menu item and availability before publishing.] #WestbourneGrove #LondonBrunch #LondonCafe"  
**Proposal angle:** A monthly set of short café stories plus event/collaboration content; their own site invites creator collaboration, so ask whether they are open to reviewing a spec concept.

### 2. SENZO — City coffee and private events
**Hook:** "A coffee break worth stepping out for — in the heart of the City."  
**15–20 sec storyboard:** street/entrance → espresso preparation → brunch detail → café atmosphere → private-event setup if approved → location end card.  
**Caption draft:** "In the City and looking for a coffee or all-day brunch stop? SENZO is at 35 Vine Street. Save this for your next visit. For private events and general enquiries, contact the team directly. [Confirm hours and current menu.] #CityOfLondon #SpecialityCoffee"  
**Proposal angle:** 8 monthly reels split across coffee, brunch, atmosphere and private events.

### 3. Alba Caffè — ethical sourcing and event catering
**Hook:** "Good food, thoughtful sourcing, and gatherings worth sharing."  
**15–20 sec storyboard:** ingredient/supplier close-ups → barista/food prep → plated catering spread → event table → CTA for catering enquiries.  
**Caption draft:** "From carefully chosen ingredients to gatherings around the table, Alba brings an ethical touch to café and event catering. Planning an office brunch or private event? Ask the team about options. [Confirm current catering menu and service area.] #LondonCatering #Whitechapel"  
**Proposal angle:** A recurring supplier/story series plus event-catering enquiry content. Only use sourcing claims verified by the client.

### 4. GetFitDXB — fitness partner and coach content
**Hook:** "A simple movement you can add to your next session."  
**15–20 sec storyboard:** trainer intro → one exercise demonstration → 2–3 coaching cues → safe modification → CTA to explore classes/partners.  
**Caption draft:** "Small, consistent steps make training easier to stick with. Save this movement cue for your next session. Ask a qualified coach to check your form and choose the right variation for you. [Coach must approve exercise, safety cues and claims.] #DubaiFitness #FitnessCommunity"  
**Proposal angle:** Coach-led educational shorts and gym-partner spotlights; never promise health outcomes.

### 5. Teilmann Properties — real estate education
**Hook:** "Before you compare Dubai properties, ask these three questions."  
**20–25 sec storyboard:** presenter hook → purpose (end-use/investment) → total costs and timeline → location/market evidence → CTA to book an advisory conversation.

**Proposal angle:** A monthly educational video series using verified market data supplied or approved by the brokerage. No investment-return promises.
