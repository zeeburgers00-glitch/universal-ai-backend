## Standard two-week pilot proposal (adapt after interest)

- 4 vertical short-form videos (15–30 seconds)
- 4 captions with CTA variants
- 1 mini content calendar
- 1 revision round per video
- Client supplies or authorizes footage, facts and brand assets
- Client approves all factual claims and final exports
- Fixed quote only after footage availability, edit complexity, language, rights and usage scope are understood
- Work starts after a written scope and any agreed deposit
- No guarantee of views, followers, bookings, leads or sales
