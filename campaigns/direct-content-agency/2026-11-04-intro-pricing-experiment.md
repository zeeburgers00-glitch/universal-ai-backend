# Founding-client pricing experiment — 2026-11-04

**Status:** Draft recommendation only. Not communicated to prospects. User approval is required before any quote is sent or accepted.

## Recommended introductory offer

### Two-week paid pilot — USD 129
- 4 vertical short-form edits, 15–30 seconds each
- Client-supplied or explicitly authorized/licensed footage
- 4 captions with hooks and calls to action
- 1 mini content calendar
- 1 revision round per video
- Excludes filming, paid advertising, posting, community management, paid-stock purchases, translation and rush delivery
- Client approves every factual statement, brand asset, usage right and final export

### Monthly starter retainer — USD 249/month
- 8 short-form edits, 15–30 seconds each
- 8 captions with CTAs
- 1 monthly content calendar
- 1 revision round per video
- Requires client-supplied/authorized footage and written scope
- Excludes filming, paid media, account management, comment/DM replies, paid stock and translation

**Positioning:** introductory remote-editing and content-packaging price, not full-service social media management. Quote only after checking actual asset availability, complexity, deadline and language. Do not accept more work than the editing capacity can reliably fulfil.

## Market reference (publicly displayed prices reviewed 2026-11-04)

- Largify's UAE packages list AED 1,499/month for 8 posts + 4 reels, AED 2,499 for 12 posts + 6 reels, and AED 3,999 for 16 posts + 8 reels; supplied footage/templates are used, while on-site shooting is quoted separately: https://www.largifysolutions.com/services/social-media-marketing
- ReelFoundry's UK price page lists £179/month for 8 reels and £379/month for 20 reels: https://reelfoundry.co.uk/pricing
- A UK freelance editor's published rate card lists £550 for an 8-video monthly bundle (standard edits): https://rjtucker-pricing.tiiny.site/

These are seller-published list prices, not independently audited market averages. Packages differ in production, number of platforms, editing complexity, language, shoot days, revisions, publishing and management. The proposed USD pricing is an entry-level experiment for client-supplied-footage editing, not a like-for-like comparison.

## Payment and scope controls

- Use a written agreement and invoice only after the client accepts the final scope.
- Suggested payment split: 50% to reserve production; 50% before clean final masters.
- Do not buy tools, stock, advertising, or subscriptions without separate approval.
- No views, follower, lead, booking or sales guarantees.
- Record income only after verified payment.
