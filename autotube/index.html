Connect your YouTube identity

Secure Google authorization lets AutoTube read your channel and use supported YouTube publishing features.

✓ Your Google password is never requested here. OAuth consent is handled by Google. Tokens must be stored encrypted on the backend.

What happens next

  1. Authorize YouTube access on Google's official consent screen.
  2. AutoTube checks whether your account already has a channel.
  3. If no channel exists, complete YouTube's official one-time channel creation flow.
  4. AI prepares channel name, niche, about text, keywords and downloadable brand assets.

YouTube Data API does not expose a general create-channel endpoint. Some channel art and profile changes require a manual YouTube Studio step.
